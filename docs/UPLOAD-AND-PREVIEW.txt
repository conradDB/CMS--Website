LUXURYTIME ATELIER — SHOPIFY UPLOAD GUIDE

Upload Luxurytime-Atelier-Shopify.zip in Shopify admin:
Online Store > Themes > Import theme (or Add theme) > Upload zip file.

Once uploaded, open Customize / Edit theme:
1. Select your approved logo in Theme settings > Logo.
2. Select your featured product collection and collection/brand tiles.
3. Check navigation menus, footer policies and newsletter settings.
4. Re-enable required app embeds and product metafield blocks from your existing theme.
5. Preview desktop and mobile, then test a real product, its variants, cart and checkout handoff.

Uploading creates an unpublished theme. Publish only through Shopify's separate Publish action when you choose to launch.

The homepage automatically uses your All products collection until a featured collection is selected. The animated watch is an illustrative design study; shopping cards use your real catalogue. The supplied macro image is illustrative, not a photograph of Luxurytime's staff or services.

The separate source ZIP is for developers. Do not upload it as a Shopify theme.
